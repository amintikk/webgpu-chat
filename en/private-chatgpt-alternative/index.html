A private ChatGPT alternative that runs on your device

If you want to use an AI without sending your conversations to any company, the most private option is to run the model on your own device. That is WebGPU Chat.

What changes compared with a cloud chat

  • Your messages never leave the browser. There is no AI server: the model answers from your GPU.
  • No account or sign-up. Open the site and start typing.
  • No cookies or analytics. The site does not track what you do.
  • Works offline once the model is downloaded. How to use it offline.
  • Free and open source. You can review the code on GitHub.

How privacy is guaranteed

The site’s security policy (CSP) only lets it connect to itself and to Hugging Face, where the models are downloaded from. Even if it wanted to, the page could not send your conversations anywhere else. Your history is stored in your browser and you can delete it whenever you like. The only thing Hugging Face sees is your IP address when you download a model, like any web server.

What to know first

  • These are small models (1.7 to 12 billion parameters): they know less and make more mistakes than large commercial models. Check what matters.
  • You need a browser with WebGPU and to download the model once (1.1 to 6.8 GB).
  • Speed depends on your graphics card. The app includes a performance test with a 0-10 score.

Which model to choose

  • LFM3 1.7B: the fastest and lightest, for everyday use and modest machines.
  • Qwen3.5 4B: the recommended balance, with a reasoning mode.
  • Gemma 4 12B: the most capable, for powerful GPUs.

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone read my conversations?

No. They are processed on your device and stored only in your browser. Neither this site nor Hugging Face receives them.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your conversations and downloaded models are deleted. You will need to download the model again to keep using it.
