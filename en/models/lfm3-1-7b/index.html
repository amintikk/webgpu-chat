What it does well

  • Fast answers even on integrated graphics.
  • Rewriting, summarising and translating short texts.
  • Extracting data and answering in a requested format, such as lists or JSON.

Its limits

  • Knows few facts about the world: easily makes up names, dates and figures.
  • Weak at multi-step reasoning and coding; the maker itself does not recommend it for that.

In our battery of 30 questions in Spanish and English it got 22 right: 8 of 10 in conversation, 7 of 10 in code (with executed tests) and 7 of 10 in reasoning.

Hardware you need

A browser with WebGPU and a GPU with shader-f16, with about 2 GB of free graphics memory. Runs well on recent laptops with integrated graphics.

Frequently asked questions

Does LFM3 1.7B work offline?

Yes. After downloading it once (1.1 GB) you can use it with no connection.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in Chrome for Android with WebGPU and in Safari 26, if the GPU supports shader-f16. It is the recommended model for phones.
