What it does well

  • More complete and accurate answers than the small models.
  • Careful writing, long summaries, coding and reasoning.
  • Very good Spanish and more than 140 languages.

Our own export, validated

There was no reliable ONNX version of Gemma 4 12B, so we exported it ourselves from Google’s official weights to 4 bits (q4f16), in files smaller than 2 GB. We then compared it with the original model in PyTorch using greedy decoding: with the same quantized weights it agrees on 97.8% of the steps (the differences are near-ties from rounding), and with the original unquantized weights on 92.3%. The model and its card are published on Hugging Face.

Its limits

  • A 7.5 GB download and a slow first load.
  • Needs a powerful GPU: on modest machines it will fail to load or run very slowly.
  • Slightly less accurate than the original because of 4-bit quantization.
  • Context limited to 4,096 tokens on this site to keep memory in check.

Hardware you need

A dedicated GPU with at least 10 GB of memory (or an Apple silicon Mac with 16 GB or more) and 16 GB of RAM. Not available on phones.

Frequently asked questions

Can my computer run Gemma 4 12B?

If you have a dedicated GPU with 10 GB or more, or an Apple silicon Mac with 16 GB, probably yes. The app checks your GPU and the performance test gives you a 0-10 score.

Does Gemma 4 12B work offline?

Yes, after downloading it once (7.5 GB).
