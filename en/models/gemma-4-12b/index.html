What it does well

  • More complete and accurate answers than the small models.
  • Careful writing, long summaries, coding and reasoning.
  • Very good Spanish and more than 140 languages.

QAT weights and fused attention

We use Google’s QAT version (gemma-4-12B-it-qat-q4_0), trained to run at 4 bits, so it loses less quality than plain quantization. The ONNX conversion comes from the community (james58899/gemma-4-12B-it-qat-ONNX) and uses fused attention (GroupQueryAttention), which is much faster on WebGPU; we only switch on integer math in its graph so prompts are read faster.

Its limits

  • A 6.8 GB download and a slow first load.
  • Needs a powerful GPU: on modest machines it will fail to load or run very slowly.
  • Slightly less accurate than the original because of 4-bit quantization.
  • Context limited to 4,096 tokens on this site to keep memory in check.

Hardware you need

A dedicated GPU with at least 10 GB of memory (or an Apple silicon Mac with 16 GB or more) and 16 GB of RAM. You can try it on phones, although memory may run out and the tab may close.

License

Frequently asked questions

Can my computer run Gemma 4 12B?

If you have a dedicated GPU with 10 GB or more, or an Apple silicon Mac with 16 GB, probably yes. The app checks your GPU and the performance test gives you a 0-10 score.

Does Gemma 4 12B work offline?

Yes, after downloading it once (6.8 GB).
