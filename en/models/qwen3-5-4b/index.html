What it does well

  • Explanations, writing and medium-complexity coding.
  • With reasoning on, it solves maths and logic problems better.
  • Good level in English and Spanish.

On the home page you will find ten of its real, unedited answers: a haiku, relativity for kids, a travel plan, Python code, a table and more.

Its limits

  • Slower than LFM3, with a download of almost 3 GB.
  • Reasoning makes answers much longer: it can take minutes on modest machines.
  • It can get specific facts wrong; check them.
  • Text only here, although the original model also accepts images.

Hardware you need

A browser with WebGPU and shader-f16, about 4 GB of free graphics memory and 8 GB of RAM or more. You can also try it on phones, although memory may run out and the tab may close.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on Qwen3.5 reasoning?

With the “Reasoning” button next to the text box, or in the settings. The model thinks before answering and you will see its reasoning folded above the answer.

Does Qwen3.5 4B work offline?

Yes, after downloading it once (2.8 GB).
